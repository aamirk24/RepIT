Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

Creating an account is quick and easy! Simply click the "Sign Up" button on our homepage, provide your email address, choose a secure password, and complete the verification process. You'll need to verify your email and provide some basic contact information to start bidding or listing items.

Yes, creating an account is completely free. However, there may be fees associated with listing certain items or when you successfully sell an item.

Bidding Process

To place a bid, navigate to the item you're interested in and click the "Place Bid" button. Enter the amount you're willing to pay, which must be higher than the current highest bid. You can also set up automatic bidding to help you stay competitive without constantly monitoring the auction.

A proxy bid allows you to set your maximum bid amount. Our system will automatically increase your bid incrementally to maintain your position as the highest bidder, up to your maximum amount. This helps you stay competitive without constantly watching the auction.

Generally, bids cannot be retracted once placed. This protects sellers from fraudulent bidding and ensures a fair auction process. Please bid carefully and only on items you're genuinely interested in purchasing.

Selling Items

To list an item, click the "Sell" or "List Item" button. You'll need to:
  • Upload clear, high-quality photos
  • Write a detailed description
  • Set a starting price
  • Choose auction duration

We charge a small listing fee for most items and a final value fee when your item sells. The final value fee is a percentage of the final selling price. Exact fees vary by item category and selling price.

Payments and Shipping

We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Each listing may have specific payment instructions from the seller. Always review payment terms before completing a purchase.

Shipping methods and costs are determined by individual sellers. Some offer free shipping, while others may charge a shipping fee. The shipping details are clearly displayed with each auction listing.

Account Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your personal and financial information. We never share your data with third parties without your consent.

If you notice any suspicious activity, immediately contact our customer support team. We have dedicated fraud prevention measures and will help investigate any potential issues.

Dispute Resolution

We have a comprehensive buyer protection program. If an item significantly differs from its description, you can open a dispute. Our team will mediate between buyer and seller to find a fair resolution.

First, communicate directly with the other party. If you can't reach a resolution, contact our customer support with details of the transaction. We'll review the case and provide guidance.

Technical Support

Our platform is fully responsive and works on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones. You can bid, list items, and manage your account from any device with an internet connection.

If you're experiencing login issues:
  • Upload clear, high-quality photos
  • Write a detailed description
  • Set a starting price
  • Choose auction duration

Legal Policies

Our full terms of service are available on a dedicated page. Key points include: users must be 18 or older, all transactions are binding, and we reserve the right to remove listings or suspend accounts that violate our policies.

Yes, our comprehensive privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. We are committed to transparency and data protection.
